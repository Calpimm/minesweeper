## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added
- Pixel-perfect remake of classic Windows Minesweeper: LED counters, face button, 3D tiles.
- Beginner, Intermediate, Expert and Custom boards with the original limits.
- Original rules: safe first click, chording, flags and question marks, 999-second clock.
- Marks (?), Color and Sound options, and local Best Times with names.
- Online leaderboard with nickname accounts and server-verified times.
- Update check against GitHub releases.
- Zoom levels, pixel-art app icon, and Homebrew installation.
